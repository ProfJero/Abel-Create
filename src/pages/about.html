My story

Who I am. I’m a Ghanaian graphic designer who fell in love with design by making flyers for friends and church events. That curiosity turned into a craft, and today it’s how I help businesses tell their story.

What I do. I design brand identities, logos, flyers, posters and social media graphics for small businesses, startups, churches and events. I also create ready-to-use PSD templates so other creatives can work faster.

What drives me. I believe good design shouldn’t be only for big brands. I’m passionate about helping local businesses look world-class, and about growing digital skills in rural communities so more young people can build careers in tech and design.

Recognition

Graphic Designer of the Year 2026

I was honoured to be named Graphic Designer of the Year at the CCTU Eminence Awards 2026. It’s a reminder that consistent, thoughtful work gets noticed, and it pushes me to keep raising the bar for every client.
