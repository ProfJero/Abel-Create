This policy explains what personal information Abel Create ("we", "I") collects when you use this website, and how it is used. It is written to follow Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, the service you need, your budget and your message.
  • Shop purchases: your email address, the files you bought and the payment reference. Payments are processed by Paystack; we never see or store your card or Mobile Money PIN.
  • Newsletter and free downloads: your email address.
  • Analytics: anonymous usage data (pages visited, device type) through Google Analytics for Firebase.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and deliver design projects.
  • To deliver the files you buy and let you re-download them.
  • To send occasional emails with new templates and tips, if you subscribed. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

Who we share it with

We only share data with the services that run this site: Google Firebase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and file delivery), Paystack (payments) and our email provider. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as you may need to re-download your files and as required for tax records. Enquiries are kept for up to two years. You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hello@abelcreate.com.
